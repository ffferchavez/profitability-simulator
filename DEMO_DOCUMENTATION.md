# Profitability Cockpit Demo Documentation

## Executive Summary
Profitability Cockpit is a real-time dashboard prototype that helps manufacturing leaders understand product margin drivers, simulate macro shifts, and triage competitive pressure in a single view.

## Business Insights
- Identify high-margin products and loss-making outliers quickly.
- Understand which cost drivers (materials, energy, labor, customs) contribute most by product.
- Quantify sensitivity to external shocks (energy prices, import fees).
- Spot competitive exposure by product to prioritize mitigation.

## Demo Script
1. Start with the header KPIs to frame revenue, margin, and margin percentage at a glance.
2. Walk through the Profitability Overview chart and call out profitable vs loss-making products.
3. Switch to the Cost Driver Breakdown to show where costs are concentrated.
4. Use the Sensitivity Simulation sliders to show the impact of energy and customs changes.
5. End with the Risk Matrix to discuss exposure to competitor pressure.
